Stations

Rename freely — appointments follow their station automatically, and any already booked will show the new name everywhere. A station holding appointments can't be removed until those are cancelled.

Dates & Hours

Dates, hours and appointment length are fixed once an event exists, so a change can't strand an appointment at a time that no longer exists. To move the schedule, create a new event.
